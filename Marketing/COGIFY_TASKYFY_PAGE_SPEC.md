### Numbered Editorial Narrative (`[ 1 ]` – `[ 4 ]`)

#### `[ 1 ] what it is`
**activity monitor, rebuilt for humans and engineers.**
macOS Activity Monitor dumps 550+ cryptic background processes like `mds_stores`, `WindowServer`, `cloudd`, and `kernel_task` into a flat table with no history and no explanation.
Taskyfy groups every helper process under the app that spawned it, explains **214+ macOS system daemons in plain English**, tells you whether each process is **Safe to Quit**, and records a **30-day SQLite WAL timeline** so you can scrub back in time to see what drained your battery or spiked your RAM 20 minutes ago.

#### `[ 2 ] why it's different`
**most system monitors use more CPU than the apps they watch.**
Taskyfy separates kernel telemetry into a read-only Mach XPC helper (`TaskyfyHelper`) using a zero-allocation **64-byte binary wire struct (`RawProcRecord`)** and automatically throttles when its window is closed:
- **`< 2.8% CPU`** steady-state footprint while live-sampling 500+ processes
- **`214+ daemons`** explained in plain English with Safe-to-Quit verdicts
- **`30 days`** of minute-by-minute SQLite WAL history on your Mac (`0 B` sent to any server)

#### `[ 3 ] how it works`
- **`01`** — **open taskyfy or press `⌥⌘T` from any screen.** View live Apple Silicon P/E core load, unified memory pressure, GPU utilization, wattage draw, and die temperatures in one minimal white workspace.
- **`02`** — **click any app or press `⌘K` to inspect.** See every child helper process, Code Signing & Notarization status, open TCP/UDP ports (`:3000`, `:5432`, `:8080`), 15-minute memory leak trails, and plain-English explanations.
- **`03`** — **take action in one click.** Quit, Force Quit, Pause/Resume (`SIGSTOP`/`SIGCONT`), renice process priority, launch your browser's internal Task Manager, or scrub 30 days of historical telemetry.

#### `[ 4 ] what you get`
- **Grouped App & Process Tree** — folds 20+ Safari/Chrome/Electron renderer helpers under their parent app with live sparklines
- **214-Daemon Plain-English Encyclopedia** — answers *"What is this process?"*, *"Why is its usage high?"*, and *"Is it safe to quit?"*
- **Apple Silicon P/E Core Matrix & GPU Telemetry** — real-time Efficiency vs. Performance core heatmaps + IOKit GPU Renderer, Tiler & VRAM utilization
- **36+ Hardware Die Sensors & Fan RPM** — direct AppleSMC + IOKit HID temperature channels for SoC, GPU, NVMe NAND, and Battery with thermal headroom tracking
- **15-Minute Memory Leak Hunter & 7-Day RAM Advisor** — detects sustained heap growth automatically and tells you whether your Mac's 8 GB / 16 GB / 32 GB RAM is enough for your workload
- **1-Click Browser Memory Relief** — calculates total RAM across Safari, Chrome, Arc, Brave, and Edge tabs and opens the browser's native Task Manager in one click
- **Live Port & Socket Radar** — see every listening TCP/UDP port (`LISTEN` / `ESTABLISHED`) mapped to its process and PID, searchable via `⌘K` (type `:3000` or `8080`)
- **Live Baseline Pinning (`Δ CPU · Δ RAM`)** — pin your current system state before running a build or test suite to measure exact CPU and memory deltas
- **Code Signing & Notarization Inspector** — verifies Apple System, Developer ID + Notarized, Ad-Hoc, or Unsigned binaries via macOS `SecStaticCode`
- **30-Day SQLite WAL History & JSON/CSV Export** — non-blocking 1-minute historical rollups stored 100% locally on your Mac
